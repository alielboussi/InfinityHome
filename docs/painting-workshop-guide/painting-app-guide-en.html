Internal painting workshop guide — Infinity Home

2) What each page is for

Home

After sign-in you only open Orders (paint jobs). The top bar has ع / EN and Alerts for push notifications.

Orders — carpentry jobs

Each card is a paint job Carpentry already sent (full order or a partial qty, for example 3 of 8). Badge shows Floor 2 WIP or finished carpentry order. “Sent by” is the carpenter.

  1. Play ▶ start or resume the timer.
  2. Pause ⏸ stop the timer without finishing.
  3. Completed items stepper (− / number / + then ✓): how many pieces of this job are painted and ready. Save with ✓.
  4. Wrench storeroom materials (paint / thinner) — storeroom 1 / 2 / 3. Floor 2 is never deducted here.
  5. Stop ⏹ type OK, then choose Delivery queue or Floor 1 storage. The whole job qty goes to that destination.

Orders — repairs

Hassan painting returns appear in the same list with a Return badge.

  1. ▶ Accept and start work.
  2. ⏸ Pause.
  3. Wrench if storeroom materials are used.
  4. ⏹ Complete — the repair joins the Delivery app Repairs queue (awaiting driver). Warehouse floors are not credited.

Partial work from Carpentry

If Carpentry marked 3 of 8 ready and sent those 3 to Painting, you only see a job of 3. Use the completed-items stepper on those 3, then Stop when the paint job is done. The remaining 5 stay on Carpentry until they send them.
