Internal carpentry workshop guide — Infinity Home

2) What each page is for

Create new item

Add a carpentry product that is not in the catalog yet. It is saved on Warehouse Floor 2 (not Floor 1) and prints a Zebra label (name, SKU, barcode, photo).

  1. Enter the base name, then pick the piece type (table, buffet, bed, and so on).
  2. Enter dimensions in cm and quantity.
  3. Photograph the piece when asked, then tap Save.

Manufacture

Grid of Floor 2 products. A normal tap does not deduct Floor 2 here — it opens a work job on Orders.

  1. Search or scan the label barcode / QR.
  2. Tap the card → enter quantity → a manufacture job starts.
  3. Long-press a card that has Floor 2 qty to send quantity to Painting (this does deduct Floor 2).

Orders — factory quotes (QT#)

Customer / showroom orders sent to carpentry. Quote number is top-right. Badges: Customer order or Showroom order.

  1. Play ▶ accept the order and start the timer.
  2. Pause ⏸ stop the timer.
  3. Line checkbox: ready qty, then send to Painting or store on Floor 1.
  4. Stop ⏹ end the order: Delivery or Floor 2 to Painting.
  5. Wrench storeroom materials used on the order.

Orders — manufacture jobs

Jobs opened from Manufacture. The timer runs while you work.

  1. ▶ Play / ⏸ Pause the timer.
  2. Wrench: add storeroom materials (scan or search) and pick storeroom 1 / 2 / 3.
  3. ⏹ Finish: if needed pick a storeroom to deduct, then Floor 1 or Delivery.

Orders — repairs

Customer returns for carpentry appear in the same Orders list (Repair badge).

  1. ▶ Accept the repair and start work.
  2. ⏸ Pause.
  3. Wrench to add materials if the repair needs them.
  4. ⏹ Complete the repair.

Search on Orders

Search by quote number QT, customer name, or phone. The list mixes quotes, jobs, and repairs.
