Painting Workshop — EAS Android keystore fingerprints (public only)
Package: online.infinityhome.painting
Firebase project: bestrest-portal-system-43108

After your first EAS Android build, run (repo root):

  eas credentials -p android

Copy the SHA-1 from the keystore, then:

  PAINTING_WORKSHOP_ANDROID_SHA1=AA:BB:... node scripts/setupPaintingWorkshopGoogleSignIn.mjs

Do NOT put keystore passwords or .jks files in this repo.
